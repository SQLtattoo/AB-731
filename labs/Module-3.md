# Module 3 Lab: AI strategy, responsible AI, and organization-wide adoption

> **Estimated time:** ~30 minutes  
> **Audience:** Business leaders and decision-makers

This lab is the third in the AB-731 exam prep series and condenses the four modules of *Transform your business with AI*—spanning prioritization, business value, responsible AI, and organization-wide scaling—into a single 30-minute hands-on session. You will use prompts in Copilot to prioritize AI opportunities, turn experiments into lasting business value, apply responsible AI principles, and plan how to scale AI across your organization, practicing the adoption-strategy thinking AB-731 tests most heavily.

Work through the four activities below in order, either on your own or with a facilitator. Each activity pairs a short concept recap with prompts you can run right now in Copilot, plus reflection questions that connect the ideas to your own role.

# Learning Objectives

- Prioritize AI use cases by value, feasibility, and time to value, and define KPIs to track results.
- Align AI strategy with business priorities and plan the organizational and culture changes adoption requires.
- Apply Microsoft's six guiding principles for responsible AI and design a lightweight governance system around them.
- Plan for AI adoption at scale, including assigning responsibilities and empowering subject matter experts to use AI themselves.
- Answer AB-731-style questions from across all three skill domains, with emphasis on "Identify an implementation and adoption strategy for Microsoft's AI apps and services" (20-25%).

## Activity 1: Prioritizing AI use cases and building the foundation

> **Estimated time:** 6 minutes

Module 1 teaches you to prioritize AI use cases by value, feasibility, and time to value, track results with clear KPIs, and put governance, security, and regulatory controls in place while unifying data and modernizing infrastructure on Azure.

### Try it: Copilot prompts

> **Prompt 1.1: Use case prioritization**
>
> List my team's top five AI opportunity ideas, then score each one on value, feasibility, and time to value, and rank them in priority order.

> **Prompt 1.2: KPI definition**
>
> For my highest-priority AI use case, suggest three measurable KPIs I could track to prove business impact within 90 days.

> **Prompt 1.3: Governance and security check**
>
> What governance, security, and regulatory controls should I confirm are in place before piloting an AI solution with [type of data]?

### Reflect

- Which of your AI ideas scores highest on both value and feasibility?
- What KPI would convince your leadership team an AI pilot is working?

## Activity 2: Turning AI experiments into business value

> **Estimated time:** 6 minutes

Module 2 focuses on turning AI experiments into dependable drivers of measurable business outcomes: aligning AI strategy with business priorities, designing a scalable and secure foundation, and integrating the organizational and culture changes adoption requires.

### Try it: Copilot prompts

> **Prompt 2.1: Strategy alignment**
>
> Here are my organization's top three business priorities this year: [list them]. For each one, suggest one AI use case that would directly advance it.

> **Prompt 2.2: Culture and change readiness**
>
> What organizational and culture changes should I plan for to help my team move from AI experiments to routine, AI-driven ways of working?

### Reflect

- Which of your current AI experiments is closest to becoming a dependable, repeatable process?

## Activity 3: Embracing responsible AI principles

> **Estimated time:** 6 minutes

Module 3 explores six principles to guide AI development and use: fairness, reliability and safety, privacy and security, inclusiveness, transparency, and accountability. It also teaches you to design a governance system that turns those principles into actionable policies and controls.

### Try it: Copilot prompts

> **Prompt 3.1: Principle self-check**
>
> Walk me through Microsoft's six responsible AI principles—fairness, reliability and safety, privacy and security, inclusiveness, transparency, and accountability—and for each one ask me a yes/no question I can use to evaluate our current AI pilot.

> **Prompt 3.2: Governance design**
>
> Help me design a lightweight governance system, including roles, review checkpoints, and escalation paths, that turns these six responsible AI principles into actionable policies for my team.

### Reflect

- Which of the six principles is your organization currently weakest on, and what's one action to strengthen it?

## Activity 4: Scaling AI across the organization

> **Estimated time:** 6 minutes

Module 4 covers full adoption of AI across an organization: setting AI strategy, assigning responsibilities, and empowering business users and subject matter experts so they can use AI themselves.

### Try it: Copilot prompts

> **Prompt 4.1: Responsibility assignment**
>
> Draft a RACI (Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed) outline for scaling an AI pilot across my department, including who owns strategy, data, risk, and day-to-day use.

> **Prompt 4.2: Empowering subject matter experts**
>
> Suggest three ways I could empower our subject matter experts to build and use AI tools themselves, without needing to go through a central IT team for every request.

### Reflect

- Who on your team is best positioned to become a subject matter expert champion for AI?
