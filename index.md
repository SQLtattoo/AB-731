# AB-731 Hands-on Labs

Hands-on labs for preparing for Microsoft Applied Skills assessment AB-731. The exercises use Microsoft Copilot prompts to connect AI concepts, Microsoft AI capabilities, responsible AI, and adoption strategy to real business scenarios.

## Labs

Complete the modules in order. Each module takes approximately 30 minutes.

1. [Module 1: Foundations of generative AI and business value](labs/Module-1.md)
2. [Module 2: Microsoft AI apps, services, and adoption decisions](labs/Module-2.md)
3. [Module 3: AI strategy, responsible AI, and organization-wide adoption](labs/Module-3.md)

## Prerequisites

- Access to Microsoft Copilot
- A browser that can open the published lab pages
- Permission to upload the supplied Excel workbooks to Copilot

## Lab assets

Module 1 uses these fictional datasets:

- [AI pilot project tracker](labs/assets/Fictional_Project_Tracker_Copilot_Lab.xlsx)
- [Customer survey feedback](labs/assets/Fictional_Customer_Survey_Feedback.xlsx)

Download the requested workbook and attach it to Copilot when instructed by the exercise.

## Using the labs

1. Open the first module.
2. Work through each activity in order.
3. Copy each prompt into Copilot and replace any bracketed placeholders.
4. Review the response and complete the reflection questions.
5. Continue to the next module.
